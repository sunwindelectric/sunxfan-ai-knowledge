### FAQ

**Q: This SF160 series product offers EC motor + 4-speed speed control. Are these 4 speeds fixed, or can they be customized to 4 different rotation speeds according to customer needs?**

A: This series supports up to 4 customer-defined speed values (i.e., up to 4 speed taps), which makes it straightforward to match the tapped-winding speed control used on AC asynchronous capacitor motors.

**Q: This SF160 series product provides 0-10V/PWM speed control. Is it an either/or choice, or are both compatible at the same time?**

A: Both 0-10V and PWM stepless speed control methods are compatible at the same time.

**Q: What are the advantages of an EC double inlet centrifugal blower compared with a single inlet blower?**

A: The EC double inlet centrifugal blower delivers greater airflow and higher static pressure because it draws in air from both sides, and when the motor is positioned in the center of the impeller it provides good impeller dynamic balance. The EC single inlet blower draws in air from only one side, which makes its structure more compact and requires less installation space, so users should choose according to the specific application scenario.
