# SUNXFAN 800 VDC EC Axial Fan in ARIDGE-A868 eVTOL

For low-altitude electric aviation, thermal management is a flight-critical system,
not an afterthought. When ARIDGE — the world's leading creator of low-altitude
mobility solutions and the largest flying-car company in Asia — finalized the
architecture of its A868 eVTOL, cooling the power electronics and battery systems
had to meet an especially demanding bar: high-voltage DC power, strict galvanic
isolation, and deterministic high-speed communication.

## Quick Answer: What Powers the A868's Cooling?

The ARIDGE-A868 eVTOL is cooled by a purpose-built **SUNXFAN<sup>®</sup> 800 VDC EC axial
fan**: it connects **directly to the aircraft's 800 VDC bus** through **dual
galvanically isolated inputs** for redundancy, exchanges speed setpoints and
diagnostics over **CAN FD**, and uses an EC external-rotor permanent-magnet
drive for software-defined, energy-efficient airflow — field-proven in real
low-altitude flight duty.

## Why a Flying Car Needs More Than a Standard Fan

Unlike ground-based equipment, an eVTOL operates across wide altitude, temperature
and vibration ranges, and its electrical architecture runs on an 800 VDC
high-voltage bus. A conventional cooling fan cannot simply be dropped in:

- **High-voltage compatibility** — the fan must interface directly with the
  800 VDC supply.
- **Isolation for safety** — redundant, galvanically isolated inputs prevent a
  single fault from cascading.
- **Deterministic control** — speed setpoints and status are exchanged over CAN FD,
  the high-speed, error-tolerant CAN variant adopted across modern vehicle networks.

## SUNXFAN's Newly Designed 800 VDC EC Axial Fan

To satisfy these requirements, SUNXFAN delivered a purpose-built EC axial fan:

- **Dual 800 VDC isolated inputs** — two independent, galvanically isolated 800 VDC
  feeds provide built-in redundancy and fault tolerance.
- **CAN FD high-speed communication** — real-time speed setpoint, monitoring and
  diagnostics over CAN FD, without compromising latency.
- **EC external-rotor axial design** — an electronically commutated
  permanent-magnet drive delivers high airflow efficiency with precise,
  software-defined speed control.

## Stable Operation on the ARIDGE-A868

The 800 VDC EC axial fan is now running stably aboard the ARIDGE-A868 flying car,
validating the design under real low-altitude flight duty. Field-proven operation
confirms:

- Reliable start-up and continuous duty on the 800 VDC bus.
- Consistent cooling performance across the flight envelope.
- Seamless integration with the vehicle's CAN FD control network.
