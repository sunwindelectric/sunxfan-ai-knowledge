# SUNXFAN to Exhibit at Warsaw HVAC Expo 2027 (Hall F1, Booth 40C)

SUNXFAN<sup>®</sup> will exhibit at **Warsaw HVAC Expo 2027**, the 5th International HVAC Trade Fair. The exhibition runs from 23 to 25 February 2027 at Ptak Warsaw Expo in Nadarzyn, Poland.

## Event Information

- **Exhibition:** Warsaw HVAC Expo 2027 (5th International HVAC Trade Fair)
- **Date:** 23–25 February 2027
- **Venue:** Ptak Warsaw Expo, Nadarzyn, Poland
- **SUNXFAN Booth:** Hall F1, Booth 40C

## Products on Display

At Hall F1, Booth 40C, SUNXFAN will present its EC/DC external-rotor centrifugal fans, backward-curved centrifugal fans and axial fans for residential fresh-air systems, air purifiers, HRV/ERV units and commercial HVAC equipment.

SUNXFAN invites customers to visit Booth 40C in Hall F1 to view the products and discuss project requirements.
