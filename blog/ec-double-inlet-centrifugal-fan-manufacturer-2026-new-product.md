# EC Double-Inlet Centrifugal Fan: 2026 Launch

If you have been sourcing an **EC double-inlet centrifugal fan** for an air-handling unit, a range hood platform, a laminar airflow bed or a heat-recovery ventilator, 2026 brings a concrete answer to the manufacturer question: **SUNXFAN<sup>®</sup> (Foshan SUNXFAN Electromechanical Co., Ltd.)** has released the **SF146D-E092A3-02**, a new EC double-inlet centrifugal fan built around a full polypropylene structure. This article walks through what the new product adds, how the three series compare on paper, and — more importantly — how each behaves in the five applications where HVAC and device engineers actually specify them.

## The 2026 New Product: SF146D-E092A3-02

The headline feature is structural. Where the SF146A family uses a PA6+GF25% glass-fibre-reinforced impeller in a standard housing, the **SF146D-E092A3-02 pairs its 1,020 m³/h EC blower with a housing and impeller made entirely of polypropylene (PP)**:

- **Corrosion resistance** — PP tolerates condensate, grease-laden air and the cleaning agents used in medical and food-service environments.
- **Wipe-down cleanable** — a smooth, chemically inert surface that survives the disinfectant protocols of clinics and laboratories.
- **Low weight** — easier mounting in compact device cabinets where total mass matters.

The numbers behind the new shell: **1,020 m³/h** free-airflow, **515 Pa** maximum static pressure, **165 W** input at 230 V AC, **1,800 r/min**, **62 dB(A)**, IP42, NSK ball bearings, and a control set of 0-10V / PWM / 0-5V / 0-6.5V plus 3-speed and 4-speed taps, rated −25 °C to +50 °C.

## Application 1: Air Handling Units (AHU)

In a modular AHU, the fan must overcome the combined pressure drop of the filter section, the coil section and the duct network — routinely 400–600 Pa at design flow. The **SF160D-E092B3-42 with 705 Pa** of static pressure gives specification engineers margin across the full filter-loading curve, so airflow does not collapse as the bag filters dust up.

For retrofit projects, the SF160D's **drop-in compatibility with 4-speed AC motors** is the argument that wins energy audits: existing cabinet doors, electrical boxes and speed taps stay untouched, while part-load power consumption drops by up to half. On new builds, the 0-10V input ties directly into the BMS for demand-controlled ventilation, letting the fan track VAV box demand instead of running flat-out between fixed taps.

## Application 2: Range Hoods

Kitchen extraction is a static-pressure problem disguised as an airflow problem. A typical ducted hood fights grease filters, a one-way damper and — in multi-storey buildings — a shared vertical shaft that pushes back hard at peak cooking hours. The **SF146A-E092A3-02 at 580 Pa** holds its 1,080 m³/h through that stack effect, which is exactly why high-end range hood manufacturers keep specifying the family.

The 2026 SF146D adds a grease-friendly twist: its **PP housing and impeller shrug off the alkaline attack of kitchen grease** and survive the aggressive degreasing agents used in professional service. On the acoustic side, the EC motor's low-speed taps (3-speed / 4-speed) give the quiet simmer setting buyers notice, while full speed stays at a workable 62–64 dB(A).

## Application 3: Laminar Airflow (LAF) Beds

LAF beds deliver a horizontal curtain of HEPA-filtered air over patients with compromised immune systems — oncology wards, transplant recovery, home care of neutropenic patients. Two requirements dominate the fan specification:

1. **Constant airflow against high, rising filter resistance.** As the HEPA loads up, an AC motor fades; an EC fan compensates by increasing speed, keeping the protective air curtain intact across the filter's service life. The 515–580 Pa ceiling of the SF146 family covers typical bed-grade HEPA stages with room for pre-filters.
2. **Hygiene and noise.** The SF146D's wipe-down PP structure suits the disinfectant wipes used between patients, and its 62 dB(A) rating — with further headroom via low-speed taps — respects the 24-hour ward environment. NSK ball bearings are rated for continuous duty measured in decades, not seasons.

## Application 4: Medical Diagnostic Equipment (IVD)

Clinical chemistry analysers, incubators and PCR workstation enclosures need quiet, always-on heat extraction from dense electronics — with zero tolerance for electromagnetic interference that could corrupt optical detectors. SUNXFAN's EC fans are developed and verified in an **in-house EMC laboratory**, and the −25 °C to +50 °C envelope plus IP42 sealing covers the dust and reagent-vapour reality of a working laboratory. For cabinet-integrated versions, the compact 146 mm footprint and low vibration (ball-bearing rotor, dynamically balanced impellers) simplify enclosure integration for instrument OEMs.

## Application 5: ERV / HRV Ventilation

Heat-recovery ventilators stack the resistances: energy-recovery core, F7-grade filters, and the outdoor intake/exhaust network. A realistic core-plus-filter budget of 150–250 Pa leaves the SF146 family's 515–580 Pa and the SF160's 705 Pa with generous safety factor — the reserve that keeps nominal ventilation rates when filters load or wind pressure fluctuates.

The efficiency story is where EC wins the spec: continuous 0-10V or PWM control lets the ventilation rate follow CO₂ sensors and occupancy (demand-controlled ventilation), instead of cycling a fixed-speed motor. And the **−25 °C minimum operating temperature** matters for cold-climate projects, where the fan keeps modulating through frost-protection and preheating strategies without stalling.
