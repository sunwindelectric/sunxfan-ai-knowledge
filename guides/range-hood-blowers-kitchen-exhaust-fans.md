# Range Hood Extractor Blowers & Kitchen Exhaust Fans

Choosing a kitchen extractor blower comes down to three things: enough airflow to clear grease and steam quickly, enough static pressure to push through grease filters and ductwork, and a motor that stays quiet and efficient. SUNXFAN<sup>®</sup>'s dual-inlet centrifugal blowers are engineered specifically for this job.

## Why dual-inlet centrifugal blowers for range hoods

- **~40% more airflow** at the same impeller diameter and speed than single-inlet fans — you get more extraction without a bigger hood.
- **High static pressure** to overcome grease filters, elbows and fire dampers in long duct runs.
- **8–12 dB(A) lower noise** than traditional single-inlet fans — critical for open kitchens.
- EC motors hold **>85% efficiency** at partial load, for up to **70% energy savings** vs AC.

## How to choose the right model

1. **Airflow (m³/h)** — match the hood width and cooking intensity. Residential hoods typically need 600–900 m³/h; commercial and island hoods 1000–1700 m³/h.
2. **Voltage** — pick by market: 24V/48V/310V DC for battery or central-DC designs, 115V AC for North America, 230V AC for Europe/global.
3. **Speed control** — 0-10V/PWM or 0-5V/0-6.5V for sensor-driven "on-demand ventilation"; 3/4-speed for simple push-button control.
4. **Noise & efficiency** — EC models are quieter and more efficient than AC at the same airflow.
