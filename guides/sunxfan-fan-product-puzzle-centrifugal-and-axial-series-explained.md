  When customers ask what SUNXFAN<sup>®</sup> actually makes, we like to describe our catalog as a <strong>puzzle</strong> — 120+ fan models across 25 series that click together into one complete thermal-management picture. Every piece is engineered and manufactured in-house in our 10,000+ m² factory, and every piece shares the same DNA: IE5-grade permanent-magnet synchronous motors with electronic commutation (EC/DC), wide-voltage power electronics, and precision speed control. The puzzle has two large corners — <strong>centrifugal fans</strong> and <strong>axial fans</strong> — and each corner holds the series your project needs. Let us walk you through it, piece by piece.
</p>

### 1. The Centrifugal Fan Family: High Static Pressure, EC Precision

Centrifugal fans are the workhorses wherever air must be pushed through resistance — filters, heat exchangers, long duct runs. SUNXFAN builds roughly 90 centrifugal models in four groups, and we confess we are proud of the depth here.

The **SF dual-inlet series** (SF133A to SF235, 24 models) is our flagship blower line. A representative piece, the [SF146A-E092A3-02](/en/products/sf146a-e092a3-02/), delivers 1080 m³/h at up to 580 Pa from a compact dual-inlet housing: 230 V AC, 180 W, 1900 r/min, and 64±1 dB(A) — with a PA6+GF25% impeller, NSK ball bearings, and both 0-10V/PWM and 0-5V control interfaces. It runs from -25 °C to +50 °C at IP42, which is why it shows up inside heat pumps, CRAC units, and air-handling equipment across Europe.

The **BC backward-curved series** is our broadest group — 47 models from BC133 to BC310. Backward-curved impellers trade a little peak airflow for materially higher pressure efficiency and quieter operation, and the frame-size coverage means we can match your duty point without forcing an oversized fan.

For tighter cabinets, the **DG single-inlet series** (25 models, DG120 to DG200) offers compact, high-pressure EC centrifugal fans — the DG140A-E072A1-01, for example, packs 380 m³/h at 460 Pa into a single-inlet format that slots into purification equipment and space-constrained enclosures.

The same centrifugal expertise extends into our **CF fan-unit series**: the duct-mounted CF315A-E092E3-01 moves 1750 m³/h at up to 1001 Pa, and remarkably supports four speed-control modes — 0-10V/PWM, 0-5V, 0-6.5V, and 3-speed — so one SKU can adapt to many controller generations.

### 2. The Axial Fan Family: High Airflow, Bus-Voltage Intelligence

Axial fans are the volume movers — when your challenge is moving a lot of air efficiently through low-resistance paths, this corner of the puzzle holds the answer. Our axial lineup spans three conventional series and one that is anything but conventional.

The **AF guard axial series** (up to 2200 m³/h, as on the AF300A-E092A3-01), **AS guard-ring series**, and **AG flange axial series** cover general ventilation, telecom-cabinet, and appliance duty with EC or DC motors and the same 0-10V/PWM control language used across our catalog.

The standout is the **800VDC axial series**, and this is where we allow ourselves some genuine pride. Models like the [AG305A-D092BA-Y1](/en/products/ag305a-d092ba-y1/) connect directly to a 480-650 V DC bus — no step-down converter, no extra weight — while delivering 4000 m³/h at up to 750 Pa. With IP68 protection, -40 °C to +85 °C operation, and CAN 2.0 / CAN FD bus communication for real-time telemetry, these fans are designed for eVTOL powertrains, ultra-fast charging infrastructure, and solid-state transformer cooling. In other words: wherever next-generation high-voltage DC systems are being built, this series was designed to be there from day one.

### 3. One Technology Platform Behind Every Piece

What makes the puzzle coherent rather than a random assortment is the platform underneath. Every SUNXFAN series — centrifugal or axial — is built around our own IE5 PMSM electronically commutated motors, covering wide-voltage formats from 8-60 VDC and 100-280 VAC single-phase through 277-480 VAC three-phase to 460-850 VDC. All models share the 0-10V/PWM control convention, are RoHS/REACH/WEEE compliant, and carry a 5-year warranty on core components. Since 2017, this platform approach has earned the trust of 800+ partners — and it is what lets us quote, customize, and ship with the consistency industrial buyers actually measure us on.

### 4. Putting the Puzzle Together for Your Project

Choosing between the families comes down to one question: is your system pressure-dominated (filters, coils, long ducts) or flow-dominated (cabinet ventilation, direct heat rejection)? If pressure — start with the SF, BC, or DG centrifugal series. If flow — start with AF, AS, or AG axial. And if your project lives on a high-voltage DC bus, the 800VDC series was engineered precisely for you.
