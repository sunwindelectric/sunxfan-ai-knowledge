# Medical & Cleanroom Ventilation Fans

Medical devices and cleanrooms have the tightest airflow requirements of any application: the fan must move air quietly, hold a stable flow rate as filters load, and run reliably around the clock. That is why low-noise, electronically commutated blowers are the standard here.

## Why low noise and precise airflow matter

- **Noise is a patient-experience and lab-acoustics issue** — the quietest SF146 model runs at just **52±2 dB(A)**.
- **Stable airflow under load** — as HEPA filters and ducts load, a fixed-speed fan loses flow; EC blowers compensate through stepless control.
- **Cleanability & protection** — IP44-rated models suit humid, washdown environments.

## How to choose the right model

1. **Noise (dB(A))** — pick the quietest model that still meets airflow; every 3 dB is roughly a halving of sound energy.
2. **Airflow stability** — 0-10V/PWM lets the fan track pressure sensors instead of running flat-out.
3. **Protection & material** — IP44 + galvanized or PP impeller for wet/humid locations.
4. **Voltage** — 24V/48V DC for portable equipment, 115V/230V AC for fixed installations.

## FFU (Fan Filter Unit) Integration

An FFU is a self-contained terminal module that pairs its own fan with a high-efficiency filter, drawing room air through the filter and discharging clean air below. SUNXFAN<sup>®</sup> works with FFU manufacturers as a component supplier — providing EC centrifugal impellers and motor assemblies rather than complete units. Because an FFU ceiling is made up of many modules running side by side, each one has to stay quiet on its own and adjust its speed as part of the group, which is exactly what EC motor technology does best.
